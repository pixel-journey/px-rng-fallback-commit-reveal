# Education

Commit-reveal is a fallback when `orng.wax` is unavailable. The house commits to a hash before the outcome path is fixed, then must reveal a preimage.

Without a bond, a losing house simply never reveals. The timeout slash moves the bond to the player so withholding is strictly worse than an honest reveal.

This lab settles the bond only. A full game would combine the revealed secret with the player's wager to decide the pot.

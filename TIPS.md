# Tips — commit-reveal bond

## Do

- Distinct player and house.  
- Positive bond; fixed timeout.  
- Slash only after deadline; settle only after valid reveal.  
- Keep mismatch non-mutating for funds.

## Don't

- Let house be player.  
- Accept non-64-hex commitments.  
- Market this as orng-equivalent without caveats.
